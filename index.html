WordSolve

Load a word list .txt file from your phone (one word per line). It is stored inside the app on this phone. Loading a file with the same name replaces that dictionary.
How to use
Tap the letters bar and type your letters. The space bar adds a blank. Backspace removes letters. Tap a tile to delete it.
The number under a tile fixes that letter to that position in the word (1 = first letter). Leave it empty to let the letter go anywhere.
Pairs: press and hold a tile until it is outlined, then tap the tile right next to it. Only words containing those two letters together, in that order, are shown. Press and hold a paired tile to remove the pair.
In the results, letters that come from a blank use the blank color, and paired letters use the pair color. The Enter key runs the search.
